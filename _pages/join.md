PhD students: The focus of our research is the modeling and theory of Bionano and 2D Materials. You should have a background in Physics / Applied Physics / Material Science / Chemistry / ECE / Math. GRE is not required. Feel free to reach out to me. You can apply to three programs at UW:

1. [Department of Physics](https://phys.washington.edu/phd-program)
2. [Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering](https://www.ece.uw.edu/academics/grad/admissions/)
3. [Molecular Engineering & Sciences Institute](https://www.moles.washington.edu/landing/phd/)

### Postdoc opening (apply now)

Background in topological materials / biomolecular simulation / quantum transport would be useful. Please send me your resume and cover letter.

### Undergraduate students

Apply with a copy of your transcript, CV and cover letter. We usually take students for EE 499 credit. Send an e-mail to Arpan De, arpan99@uw.edu.
